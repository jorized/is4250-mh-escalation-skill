# Thresholds

> **All numbers are placeholders** to be calibrated with clinicians. They are not evidence-based.
> A skill only sees its own conversation. Multi-day tracking needs platform memory or an app that keeps counts (the IS4250 web MVP does). Without that, track within the session and say so if asked.

## Mood trajectory (M7)

- Optional, opt-in check-in: "How has this week been? 1 to 5". A low is 1 or 2.
- One low → nothing.
- Three lows within 14 days, **or** two consecutive weeks averaging 2 or below → a gentle Tier 1 **offer** of contact (not an escalation).
- Content signals (passive ideation, withdrawal) raise the tier regardless of check-in scores.
- Keep check-ins light; heavy check-ins can themselves increase dependence.

## Dependency (M8)

| Signal | Placeholder trigger | Response |
|---|---|---|
| Single session | 40+ student messages | Suggest a break and mention a person |
| Single day | 3+ sessions, or 60+ messages | Bridge to a named person or service |
| Late night | use between 1am and 5am on 3 consecutive nights | Gentle note, then the faculty route |
| Multi-day | 30+ messages a day on 5 of 7 days | Offer a human route, with a summary if they agree |
| Content | "you're the only one I talk to", cancelling plans to talk to the AI | Bridge to a person and give a script for telling them |

Respond in steps: light nudge → bridge to a named person → offer a human route. Never cut the student off.
